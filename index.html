The Campus

Seroma Christian High School sits on the rolling hills of Mukono, Uganda, founded in 2002 by Lt. Col. Robert and Mrs Margaret Sekidde. Wisdom Hall, the terracotta blocks, the stone terraces and the Jubilee Pitch are all here — recreated in 3D.

Every scene in the league is illustrated as a stylized 3D render of Seroma — the orange blocks, green roofs, Wisdom Hall, the track and the pool.

How to play

Same controls on phone and desktop

⚽ Football

  • W A S D (or left thumb) — move around the pitch
  • Mouse / drag right side — look around
  • Hold click or F — power up, release to kick where you look
  • Look at the top corner and hold longer for a rising shot into that corner
  • Look at a teammate and release to pass
  • Shift (or push the stick fully) — sprint
  • Space / CALL — call for the ball
  • C / TACKLE — slide in to win it back
  • 1 / 2 / 3 (or Q / E / R) — step-overs, rainbow flick, roulette
  • Right click / LOOK BACK — look over your shoulder at the press
  • P / Esc — pause

🏀 Basketball

  • Move — left thumb stick / WASD
  • Aim at the rim, then hold & release in the green zone
  • Step behind the arc for a 3-pointer
  • Perfect release adds a bonus point

🏊 Swimming

  • Tap STROKE (or Space) when the marker enters the bright band
  • Perfect rhythm keeps your speed up
  • KICK (C) for an underwater burst at the cost of stamina
  • Drift left/right with the stick to hold your lane

📱 Tips

  • Landscape gives you a wider pitch view
  • Tap ⏸ any time to pause or quit
  • Sound on for the stadium roar
